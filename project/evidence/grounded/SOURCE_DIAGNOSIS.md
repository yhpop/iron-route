## 1. Clean ground: actual causal chain

- `main.js:143` chooses `appearance:'battlefront'` before ruined/scorched/crossfire choices. It selects `createCrossfireWarfieldView`, but that function immediately returns `createBattlefrontDepotGroundView` for battlefront. Older soot/scars/outside-ruin buckets are therefore not produced.
- `terrain-candidate/relief-view.js:74–86` replaces the whole crossfire palette with mineral soil. Ash is `smoothstep(.36,.74,B)` and its mix caps at `.48`; there is **no dark soot branch**. Crossfire's separate branch contains `warChar` and stronger soot but is not selected. Hard asphalt still blends over ground by R, so broad mineral weather barely changes road surfaces.
- B still comes from `crossfireWeatherWeight`: warped broad centers approximately (-26,4), (23,-1), (0,-18), not from the new warehouse/loading-damage composition. The mask remains 1024² RGBA; R asphalt coverage, G earth-route coverage, B weather, A earth tracks/asphalt weather. Same six PBR maps, same 1-draw relief.
- `battlefront-depot-ground-view.js` gathers only `ruinInstanceId` movement spans. Its strongest foot stain is .40 alpha, tapers within `1.65*(1+.16*sin(...))` metres of span edges, and is zero wherever road `edge<.65`. It is excellent grounding, not sufficient district-scale damage. `south-depot` is absent from these sources. The 340 tiny random triangles (.045–.195 m scale) are explicitly optical paint and cannot communicate substantial rubble or landscape disturbance. Adding more is the wrong repair.

### Preferred strict repair: sealed derelict storage shell

The existing full box remains solid to movement AND ordinary projectile rays. Replacing it by the open `workshop`/`broken-corner`/`window-wall` assets would leave invisible blocking mass. Removing the roof exposes a collision-filled cavity; carving a visual hole is equally false. Genuine collapsed traversable ruin would require changing collision semantics and is rejected here.

For a strict visually enclosed existing volume, replace **only its view**, selected by `c.id==='south-depot'`, with original low-poly closed architecture exactly covering the prism above: continuous masonry exterior, closed/boarded loading shutters flush in the facade, and closed charcoal/ash cap at y=4. No cavity, transparent window, gap, jagged notch into the volume, or protruding pile that suggests extra solid cover. Use borrowed existing Brick/Concrete/ZincRoof texture families with candidate-owned desaturated/burned material clones; do not map the ruin atlas onto arbitrary fresh geometry (its UV islands were baked per ruin). Coherent soot rises from the loading apron and fades onto the roof; seam, fracture and scorch marks are surface treatment, not fake openings. Derelict/abandoned, **not collapsed** is the honest design label. ≤256 triangles,≤3 material buckets; replaces7,156 triangles/8 primitives, so funds the apron and decreases architecture complexity.

A cheaper alternative retains the current warehouse geometry and selectively desaturates/soots its roof/walls using candidate-owned shader/material variants; this removes the green/new look but leaves its recognizable intact sawtooth silhouette and the original coarse box-versus-roof-envelope approximation. That is acceptable only if “derelict” is enough; it is NOT an exact new enclosure of the full collision box. The strict option above is therefore preferred under the stated requirement.
