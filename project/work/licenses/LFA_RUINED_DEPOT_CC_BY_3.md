# Ruined depot: attribution and changes

**Geometry:** “Destroyed Walls - 3D Models” by **LFA**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/destroyed-walls-3d-models
Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/destroyedwalls_by_lfa.zip

Selected source groups: polySurface1011, polySurface773 and polySurface806.
Changes: selected three groups from the original collection; translated footprint
centres and base origins; applied 0.8 metres per source unit; converted Blender
Z-up to glTF Y-up; preserved all 757 source triangles; authored a new shared
padded UV atlas and concrete material; exported an embedded GLB. Original UV1
and UV2 survive in the packed authoring evidence. No endorsement is implied.

**Colour source only:** “Destroyed City Assets” by **Fleurman**, CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/destroyed-city-assets
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The acquired `Ciment_brisé.png` is retained byte-for-byte in `originals/ruined-depot`.
No Fleurman structural mesh is used. Its colour is reprojected/baked into the
new atlas; the atlas itself is not claimed to be the unchanged original image.

Roughness and tangent normal are deterministic artist-authored approximations
from Blender noise and baking, not measured or scanned PBR data. License pages
saved with the previously acquired sources are packaged as provenance; no new
network access occurred. See manifest and source checkpoint for hashes.
