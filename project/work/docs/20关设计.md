# 20 关设计说明（初始设计目标）

这是关卡的设计依据，不是实测耗时或玩家验收。最终输入回放与当前验证范围见包根目录《验收与复用说明》。

## Rules the content can actually use
Tank acceleration, braking, reverse and independent turret aim are unchanged. Solid cover blocks bodies and shots. Rubble changes traction. Destructible barriers really disappear. Turrets have fixed armor arcs, charge telegraphs, range and damage. Trucks drive real routes, wait when the tank is too far away and physically arrive. Reach, disable, breach, escort and defend are implemented; per-entity objective activation is also implemented. No hacking, bridges raising, water physics, stealth, moving enemy tanks, ammo pickups or environmental explosions are assumed.

No new mechanics are required. Visual water is always outside playable space or enclosed by solid retaining walls; a painted polygon is never a collision claim. A trigger is described as arriving at a rendezvous, not as a nonexistent interaction. All mission timers are estimates, not compulsory speed tests. A player may stop behind cover and think. Enemy charge times start forgiving and never require touch-screen snap aim.

## Content and renderer contract
`campaign/src/content/crafted20/` owns all new records and a separate provider. Stable IDs are `crafted20-001`…`crafted20-020`. Each has a `design` object describing spatial identity, sequence, alternative, beat, pacing target, safe planning positions, evidence limits and proposed mechanics (empty). `sceneDressing` declares district, roads, collision-bound landmark assets, non-colliding props and enclosed/off-map water. Rendering must not add invisible blockers or replace collision dimensions with arbitrary mesh bounds. Roads reference authored world-space points. Non-colliding props belong outside the intended driving/firing envelope. The asset integrator resolves stable keys to GLBs.

## Verification and acceptance
First three must pass schema/dependency, hull+barrel sweep, reachable firing position, shield-angle and real truck routing checks before the other seventeen are authored. Then apply the same checks to all twenty, including alternate routes and no-spawn-fire/covered planning positions. These checks do **not** establish fun, balance, time-to-complete, mobile readability or human playtesting. Those remain explicitly unverified. Do not market a CPU witness as an actual playthrough. Art/control integration is separate and must be reviewed before publishing.
