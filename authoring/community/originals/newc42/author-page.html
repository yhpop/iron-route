Hi! Thanks for checking out my asset pack!😁

I've made those low poly models to learn blender and decided to put them out for you for free under a CC0 license, which means you can use them in any project without attribution. 

The asset pack contains three low poly models of German tanks from world war two. All parts of the tanks, such as wheels, turrets, and guns, are separate, movable objects. They also use one color palette as a texture.

  • Leichttraktor - 5 645 verts
  • Pz-III-J - 5 105 verts
  • Pz-VI-Tiger - 5 175 verts

The models are available as .blender, .fbx and .unitypackage.
